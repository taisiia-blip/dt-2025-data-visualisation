When the people of Kungla, in golden times,
once sat down to eat,
then Vanemuine, on the meadow,
began to play the story of the kannel.

But he went to play in the forest,
he went to the fields with his song.

From there came the bird and the leafy tree
and the animals’ singing tale;
then sang the forest and the sea’s mouth
and the kin of the Estonian people.

Then a beautiful melody sounded
and garlands were placed on heads.
And the daughters of the meadow mothers
were seen by the Estonian people.

I sing on the moss, on the hill
and late in the evening outdoors
and the sound of Vanemuine’s kannel
beats within my chest.
